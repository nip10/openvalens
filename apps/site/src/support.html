Help

Support

Write to hello@openvalens.com from the email address of your account. We reply on working days, usually within two. Tell us whether you use OpenValens Cloud or your own server, and which app (web, iPhone or Android) and version.

OpenValens cannot help with medical questions. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your health. In an emergency, call 112 (or your local emergency number).

Signing in

  • Email link. Enter your email and open the link we send. Links expire after a short time and work once; if one has expired, ask for a new one. Check your spam folder if nothing arrives within a few minutes.
  • Google or Apple. Use the same method you created the account with. If you chose “Hide my email” with Apple, your account uses Apple's relay address.
  • The app asks for a server. Choose OpenValens Cloud for a Cloud account, or enter the address of your own server.

Subscription and invoices

The workspace owner manages the subscription on the web, in Settings › Plan & billing: plan, trial, card, invoices and cancellation. Invited members are covered by the owner's plan and never pay.

Export your data

In Settings › Export records, prepare a complete export of your account and download it as a ZIP file when it is ready. You can also download the clinical record as JSON and single sections as CSV or PDF.

Delete your account

You can delete your OpenValens Cloud account and all of its data at any time, in any subscription state:

  1. Sign in to the app or to app.openvalens.com.
  2. Open Settings and choose Delete account and all data.
  3. Confirm by typing the requested text. You may be asked to sign in again first.

What is deleted: your account and everything recorded about you (health records, photos, AI conversations, sessions and push tokens), and every workspace you own with everything in it. Your memberships in other people's workspaces end. An active subscription is cancelled. If another person also owns one of your workspaces, the app asks you to remove them or transfer the workspace first.

What is kept: invoices, which Stripe keeps for 10 years under Portuguese tax law; ids-only security and erasure records; and backups, which are overwritten within 14 days. If a backup is ever restored, your deletion is applied again.

If you cannot sign in, write to hello@openvalens.com from the email address of your account and ask us to delete it. We confirm it is you and delete it within one month.

To stop syncing without deleting anything, remove OpenValens's access in Apple Health or Health Connect, and sign out of the app.

Privacy requests

For access, correction, objection or any other request under the GDPR, write to hello@openvalens.com. The privacy policy explains what we collect and your rights.

Self-hosting

If you run your own server, the install guide and self-hosting docs cover setup, backups and upgrades. Report bugs and ask questions in GitHub issues. Your server's operator, not OpenValens, is responsible for the data on it.

Security issues

Please do not report vulnerabilities in public issues. Follow the security policy.
