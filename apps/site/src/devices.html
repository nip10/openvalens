Supported devices

The OpenValens app for iPhone and Android talks to these watches, rings, scales and sensors directly over Bluetooth. It never asks for your account with the device’s maker, and your records go to your OpenValens server, not to theirs. What syncs depends on the device: steps, heart rate, sleep, weight or workouts. The app shows what a device sends once you connect it.

Some Amazfit, Xiaomi and Fossil watches need their pairing key, which you get once from the maker’s account. The app links to a guide when it asks for one.

Direct Bluetooth support is new. No model has been tested on a real device yet, so each model below shows how far it is proven. The list is generated from the app’s code.
