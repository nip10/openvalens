Why OpenValens

What sets us apart

A closed app asks you to trust code you can’t read, on servers you don’t choose. OpenValens shows its work.

  • Open source and yours

    The code is public under the GNU AGPL, and Cloud runs the same code. Export everything as JSON, CSV or PDF, delete it at any time, or run the app on your own server. No lock-in.

    “Open” means open source. It never means your records are public: every workspace is invite-only.

  • Your devices, without their cloud

    Many Garmin, Huawei, Amazfit and Pebble watches, and rings, scales and heart-rate straps, sync straight to the OpenValens app over Bluetooth. No account with the maker, and your records go to your OpenValens server, not to theirs.

    Ask Coach about your own records and what changed in them. On your own server, run it on Anthropic or any OpenAI-compatible service, including a model on your own machine. Cloud includes it, with a monthly cap.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is it really open source?

Yes. The server, the web app and the mobile app are public on GitHub under the GNU AGPL v3.0 or later, and every release is tagged. You can read the code, run it on your own server and change it. Cloud runs the same code.

Can you read my data?

On Cloud, your records are encrypted in transit but not end to end. Only authorised staff can reach the production systems, only to run and secure the service, and we look at your account only at your request or to investigate abuse or a security incident.

Your records are never sold or used for advertising. There is no tracking; usage analytics on Cloud is optional, off until you turn it on, and never includes health data. The privacy policy has the details.

On your own server, your records stay in its database, with no published port. Resend sends sign-in and invitation emails and Let’s Encrypt issues the HTTPS certificate. AI, barcode lookup, S3 storage, push and single sign-on stay off unless you turn them on.

Is it medical advice?

No. OpenValens keeps your records and shows what changed in them. It is a general-wellness app for your own records, not a medical device, and the AI coach can be wrong. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about anything that worries you.

What happens if you shut down?

If we ever stop offering Cloud, we give at least 90 days’ notice, refund any prepaid time you have not used, and keep export available until the end of the notice period (terms). The app is open source, so it does not disappear with us: you, or anyone, can keep running it.

Intended purpose

OpenValens is a personal record-keeping and general-wellness app. It helps you store, organise and view your own health-related records (such as lab results you enter or import, body measurements, workouts, nutrition, sleep and wearable data) and track healthy habits. It shows publicly available guidance published by recognised public-health authorities, attributed and unaltered, for your information. OpenValens is not a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, screen for, prevent, monitor, predict, treat or alleviate any disease or condition, and it does not provide medical advice. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your health.
