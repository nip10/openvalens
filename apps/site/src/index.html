What it keeps for you

  • Sleep

    Nights, stages and your trend.

  • Activity

    Workouts, and weekly minutes beside the WHO range.

  • Heart and vitals

    Heart rate and vitals over time.

  • Body

    Weight and body measurements.

  • Meal diary

    USDA and Open Food Facts catalogs, or your own foods.

  • Labs

    Each marker’s readings together.

  • Export and delete

    JSON, CSV or PDF, or delete everything you own.

  • Sharing

    Invite-only workspaces with roles.

  • AI assistant

    Optional and off by default. It is not medical advice and can be wrong.

Where your data goes

Your records live in your instance’s database, with no published port. There is no analytics and no telemetry.

“Open” means open source (AGPL-3.0). It never means your records are public: every workspace is invite-only.

  • Resend sends sign-in and invitation emails.
  • Let’s Encrypt issues the HTTPS certificate.
  • Off unless you turn them on: AI, barcode lookup, S3 storage, push and single sign-on.

Intended purpose

OpenValens is a personal record-keeping and general-wellness app. It helps you store, organise and view your own health-related records (such as lab results you enter or import, body measurements, workouts, nutrition, sleep and wearable data) and track healthy habits. It shows publicly available guidance published by recognised public-health authorities, attributed and unaltered, for your information. OpenValens is not a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, screen for, prevent, monitor, predict, treat or alleviate any disease or condition, and it does not provide medical advice. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your health.
