# OpenValens trademark policy

The code is free software under AGPL-3.0-or-later. The licence covers the
code, not the name: section 7(e) of the AGPL lets us decline to grant
trademark rights, and we do. This policy says how you may use the
**OpenValens** name and logo (the "marks"), so users can tell the official
project apart from other versions.

## Fine without asking

- Saying truthfully what your thing relates to: "works with OpenValens",
  "OpenValens integration", "a fork of OpenValens".
- Redistributing **unmodified** official releases under the same name.
- Community, educational, research and press use.
- A "Powered by OpenValens" link on a self-hosted instance.

## You must use a different name for

- Modified builds and forks.
- App-store builds that are not built from an official release tag.
- Any hosted, multi-tenant or paid service you offer to others. You may say it
  is "based on OpenValens".
- App names, package identifiers and domain names containing the marks.

## Ask first

- Using the logo outside the unmodified app.
- Merchandise and event names.

## Never

- Implying the project or its maintainers endorse you.
- Attributing medical or clinical claims to the project.

## Contact and enforcement

Questions or permission requests: open a discussion or issue on the
OpenValens repository. If we see a problem, we will ask politely first.
