## Notes for #307 (store labels and Data safety)

- **Collected and linked to the person:** email, name (optional), health and
  fitness data, photos (progress photos, user-provided), user content (notes,
  AI chats). Purchases: none in the app (subscriptions are bought on the web)
  `[CONFIRM: how Apple treats web-only purchases for the label]`.
- **Not collected:** location, contacts, browsing history, advertising ids,
  audio. Camera frames for barcode scanning are not kept.
- **Diagnostics:** crash data (Sentry), not linked to the person.
- **Tracking:** none. No data used for advertising; no data sold.
- **Encryption in transit:** yes. **Deletion:** in-app (Settings › Delete
  account and all data) and on request.
- When the app is connected to a self-hosted server, the operator collects
  the data, not us; the labels describe the store app with Cloud.
