## Highlights

- **Custom trackers.** Keep a record of anything the app does not cover, such
  as meditation minutes, a 1–5 mood scale or a daily count. Trackers are
  private unless you share one with your workspace. Insights say what changed
  in a tracker, with weekly totals for trackers you log a few days a week.
- **Goals on your trackers.** Set a goal on one of your trackers, such as 10
  minutes of meditation a day or a streak of logged days. These goals count
  toward your goal progress.
- **Trackers filled from your phone.** Link a Meditation tracker to mindful
  minutes in Apple Health or mindfulness sessions in Health Connect, and on
  iOS 18 or later a Mood tracker to your daily State of Mind. The app asks for
  access only when you link one, and only reads.
- **Log in a few taps.** A "+" tab in the app and a command palette on the web
  open one list for every kind of entry: water, weight, blood pressure, sleep,
  supplements, drinks, pain and your trackers. Type a one-liner such as
  "500 ml" or "weight 82.4", on the web or in the app. Long-press the app icon
  for shortcuts to the entries you log most.
- **Pain on the body map.** Log where it hurts, how much and when, on the web
  and in the app.
- **Drinks, caffeine and alcohol.** Drink presets in the meal diary, opt-in
  caffeine and alcohol totals, and the time of your last caffeine next to your
  sleep.
- **A new Food tab in the app:** a diary timeline, a week strip, a trend card,
  copy, move and delete across days, diet-style presets, protein per kg and a
  weekly check-in on your nutrition targets.
- **A new Training tab in the app:** a next-up card, routines with a
  full-screen editor, an active workout with one exercise per screen, and an
  exercise library with images. Every server now sets up the exercise catalog
  and its images by itself.
- **Connect your own AI app.** Point an AI app such as Claude or ChatGPT at
  your own records, signed in with OAuth or a personal key. If you allow it,
  it can add entries. Meals, lab results and workouts with exercises it does
  not know wait as drafts until you check them, on the web or in the app.
- **More devices, without their cloud.** The app now connects over Bluetooth
  to many more watches, bands, rings and scales, including Garmin, Huawei and
  Honor, Zepp OS, older Amazfit and Mi Band models, Xiaomi, Pebble, Fossil
  Hybrid HR, Casio, Colmi rings, Bangle.js and PineTime, and standard heart
  rate, blood pressure and temperature sensors. On Android, some Garmin,
  Huawei, Honor and Zepp OS models also sync over Bluetooth Classic. SpO₂ and
  respiration rate are new metrics.
- **More on the timeline:** water, supplements taken and workouts.
- **An easier first run:** three short screens on the web and in the app, a
  "Finish setting up" card, one place to connect a device, and height asked
  only when the energy estimate needs it.
- **What's new:** a card like this one after each update, and a public
  changelog with an Atom feed at openvalens.com/changelog.
- **Numbers the way you write them:** every number field accepts a decimal
  comma, and a number that reads two ways, such as "1,250", asks which one you
  meant.
- **Fixes:** the app stays readable at the largest text sizes on small phones,
  marking a supplement as taken works offline, and the Android app is smaller.

OpenValens is a personal record-keeping and general-wellness app. It is not a
medical device and does not give medical advice.

## Self-hosting: upgrade notes

- Upgrade: `git fetch --tags && git checkout v1.2.0 && deploy/setup.sh upgrade`
  ([Upgrading](https://github.com/nip10/openvalens/blob/v1.2.0/docs/self-hosting/upgrading.md)).
- Migrations: 28, all additive (new tables and columns, and wider check
  constraints for the new device families, drink entries, AI app entries and
  tracker goals), from `20261106120000_nutrition_energy_rate` to
  `20261115093700_goal_source_tracker`. The upgrade takes a pre-migrate backup
  first.
- New or changed settings:
  - `MCP_ENABLED` turns on connected AI apps (the MCP server at `/api/mcp`,
    the REST tool API and OAuth sign-in for AI apps). Off on a self-hosted
    server unless set to `true`; on in Cloud. Limits:
    `MCP_RATE_LIMIT_PER_GRANT_PER_MINUTE` (60),
    `MCP_RATE_LIMIT_PER_MEMBER_PER_MINUTE` (120), `MCP_WRITE_ENTRIES_PER_CALL`
    (100) and `MCP_WRITE_ENTRIES_PER_DAY` (1000). See
    [Connected AI apps](https://github.com/nip10/openvalens/blob/v1.2.0/docs/ai-apps.md).
  - `EXERCISE_CATALOG_ASSETS_ENABLED` is gone: the exercise catalog and its
    images now set themselves up once, on the first start. That one-time setup
    needs outbound HTTPS to `wger.de` and `raw.githubusercontent.com`; it
    retries until it finishes.
- Breaking changes and manual steps: none for the supported install. Behind
  your own proxy, route `/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource` and
  `/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server` (and the paths under them) to the
  server, as `deploy/Caddyfile` does, if you turn on `MCP_ENABLED`.
- Mobile app: server API version 2, unchanged. The store app 1.2.0 connects to
  servers from 1.1.0 on; update the server to 1.2.0 first, so the new screens
  (trackers and their goals and phone feeds, pain, drinks, AI app drafts)
  work. The goal-progress score moves to `goal-progress-0.2.0`: tracker goals
  now count toward it ([Score algorithm](https://github.com/nip10/openvalens/blob/v1.2.0/docs/score-algorithm.md)).
