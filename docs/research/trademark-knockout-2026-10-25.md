## Risk note (factual, not legal advice)

- **OPENVALENS:** no identical or near-identical mark in TMview (EU, WIPO, the national offices TMview covers, including INPI PT) or USPTO, in any class.
- **VALENS alone, live, in classes 9, 42 and 44.** "VALENS" is a crowded element: dozens of unrelated owners hold it worldwide. The closest live marks to "a self-hosted health tracking app" are:
  1. **Valens Tecnologies, Inc.**: EUTM 019361448 (registered 2026, classes 9 and 42) and US 99425415 (pending, 9 and 42). Identical word, same goods type (downloadable software, mobile apps, SaaS). Its specification is aimed at metaverse, digital wallet and NFT software, not health, but class 9/42 software terms are broad. This is the nearest overlap on goods.
  2. **Stiftung Kliniken Valens** (Swiss rehabilitation clinics): CH 00349/2026 and international registration WO 1941209 for "valens" in classes 9, 16, 35, 41, 42, 43, 44, plus several "Kliniken Valens" composite marks. Same field (health). Which countries WO 1941209 designates was not checked.
  3. **Valens Semiconductor Ltd.**: VALENS in class 9 in the US (79109772), WIPO (1108361), UK, Israel and China. Goods are integrated circuits and communications hardware, not software or health. Same class, different goods.
  4. **Eyemed Technologies Ltd.**: VALENS in the EU (018867401), US (97898702), UK (also class 9), Canada, Israel and China, for intraocular lenses and eye-surgery services (classes 10 and 44). Health field, but devices and surgery, not software.
  5. **Valens Health** (NZ 1335960, filed, class 44 "Healthcare") and **VALENS NUTRI** (EUTM 019005291, classes 5, 42, 44). Health or nutrition services under VALENS-led names.
- The "Open" prefix and the combined single word "OpenValens" make the mark as a whole differ from these. All of the above live marks share the dominant "VALENS" element, though, and two of them (Valens Tecnologies for software, Kliniken Valens for health) sit in the same classes as OpenValens. Expect these to come up if OpenValens is ever filed as a trade mark or opposed. Using "Valens" alone as a module prefix (Valens Labs, Valens Move) is closer to these marks than "OpenValens" is.
- No PT-specific VALENS mark was found.

## Limits

- **TMview blocked further requests partway through.** After the result lists were downloaded, the EUIPO firewall began returning a "Problem detected" page ([screenshot](trademark-knockout-2026-10-25/tmview-blocked.jpg)) when about 50 detail records had been read. So:
  - goods/services text marked "n/r" was not retrieved;
  - no screenshot of the TMview VALENS results list could be taken (the list data comes from TMview's own search API in the same browser session);
  - the designated countries of the WIPO registrations (1108361, 1941209) were not checked.
- USPTO TSDR returned "429 Too Many Requests" for record detail, so US goods/services are taken from the search-result summaries, which are truncated.
- TMview was searched for the name containing the string, with no phonetic or fuzzy matching. Variants such as VALENZ, VALENCE or VALIENS were not searched in TMview (VALENZ appears only because USPTO returned it).
- EUIPO eSearch, WIPO Global Brand Database and the INPI PT site were not searched separately. TMview covers EUIPO, WIPO and INPI PT data.
- Other classes (for example 5, 10, 35) were not analysed beyond what appears in the tables.
