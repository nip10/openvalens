## Corrected claim recommendations

- **Onboarding:** Keep “no ideal number/length or proven skippability effect established in reviewed sources.” The 2026 chronic-pain setup study (48 participants; nonrandomized) is a narrow adoption signal, not a consumer-app onboarding recipe or evidence for sustained retention. Accessibility reviews support testing barriers and inclusive design, not the causal efficacy of save/resume or progressive disclosure. (See [JMIR 2026 record](https://mhealth.jmir.org/2026/1/e78827), [Janssen et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8754328/).)
- **Demographic inputs:** Mifflin’s source is an adult derivation cohort with binary sex-coded equation term, not proof that age/sex/height/weight deliver individualized energy estimates or that gender identity maps to the coefficient. Recommend just-in-time inputs only for a clearly specified estimate; label it predictive and suppress when required inputs are absent. Do not store exact DOB absent a separate need. Absence of reviewed validation is not proof no suitable inclusive equations exist.
- **Age/minors:** Adult derivation evidence does not transfer to minors. Do not serve adult equation outputs to minors absent pediatric-specific validation and policy. This is a conservative scope boundary, not a claim that no pediatric evidence exists.
- **HRV/load:** HRV-guided endurance-training evidence concerns training interventions in small athlete samples and endpoints; it does not validate a consumer daily-readiness number, generalize injury risk, or establish universal thresholds. Preserve metric, device, protocol, and time window; present observed values/trends descriptively pending target-population validation.
- **Sleep/activity:** Device validation is device/algorithm/metric/cohort-specific. PSG agreement does not make sleep stages ground truth or validate readiness; steps are distinct from estimated energy expenditure. Do not combine unlike vendor outputs as a calibrated common scale.
- **Composite validity:** Current recommendation should be “do not make clinical/physiological claims or lock production weights/cutoffs absent validation,” not “composites are proven invalid.” The Doherty bibliographic record matches, but detailed count/findings remain incompletely checked here because publisher access failed. Independent validation status is unknown from this audit, not universally nonexistent.
- **Self-report and goal progress:** Athlete self-report findings and PROMIS fatigue psychometrics concern defined populations/constructs and recall periods; they do not validate a daily general-adult readiness total. User-target progress can be reported as transparent arithmetic over recorded data, not as a validated health index.
- **Decision timing:** Numerical composite decisions warrant delay if they imply calibrated readiness, cross-device/manual equivalence, or actionable thresholds. A clearly marked experimental/descriptive prototype may be considered only with explicit construct, missingness/provenance rules, user research, and prospective validation plan. Component trends and transparent goal-specific arithmetic need not be blocked.

## Exclusions and unresolved gaps

- Exclude the unverified 2026 “step goal personalization strategies” source already flagged in the notes until its primary record and findings are checked.
- Do not use marketing, influencer commentary, or vendor score explanations as efficacy/clinical-validity evidence.
- Exact effect estimates from onboarding/goal-setting sources should be quoted only with their population, design, endpoint, and certainty attached; do not promote associations or borderline/selective outcomes to causal general claims.
- This bounded review did not independently inspect primary full text for every source already well-covered in the notes (sleep-tracker, activity-device, PROMIS, accessibility, or engagement reviews). Their note citations are not re-certified here. Check primary records before reproducing detailed statistics in final publication.
- No exhaustive search was performed to establish that no general-adult composite, inclusive energy equation, or optimal onboarding evidence exists. Correct formulation: “not identified/validated in the reviewed sources,” not “none exists.”
- Publisher page for the 2025 wearable composite review returned 405 in this audit; full-text item counts and individual score-validation claims remain unresolved. Retain only cautious product-review context or omit detailed counts pending full text.
